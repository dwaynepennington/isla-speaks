    Pictures

    Tap Photo to use your own picture instead of the cartoon, such as Bluey or Mickey on your TV, or a real photo of Mama, Papaw, or Bryson. Real photos are often the easiest for her to recognize. Tap Cartoon to switch back. Uncheck any picture Isla doesn’t need yet; fewer choices can be easier when she’s starting out.

    Tips

    • When Isla taps a picture, answer her right away, even if you think she tapped it by accident. Quick answers teach her the buttons work.
    • Use the board yourself as you talk: tap “Bath time” as you say it. Showing her is the fastest way she learns.
    • The colored borders group the pictures: pink for signs, orange for needs, yellow for feelings, purple for rest, blue for things to do, and green for people.
    • Keep pictures in the same spots so she can find them by memory. Hide pictures rather than moving them.
    • To keep her inside the app on an iPad or iPhone, turn on Guided Access (Settings, Accessibility, Guided Access), then triple-click the side or home button.
